The WAYMARK approach

Guidance that starts with your reality.

Students have different qualifications, budgets, locations and ambitions. The goal is to make guidance relevant to those differences instead of offering the same generic list to everyone.

Understand your situation

Build a picture of your current education level, qualifications, interests, preferences and practical constraints before evaluating opportunities.

Planned capability

Explore real opportunities

Build a traceable information layer for universities, colleges, courses, admission processes, counselling and career opportunities.

Data integration in development

Match with your conditions

Compare available requirements against your actual profile. Explain why an option fits, what remains uncertain and which alternatives may be worth exploring.

Planned capability
Trust before technology

Useful answers mean nothing without reliable information.

A convincing answer can still be wrong. WAYMARK is being designed around evidence, source attribution and explicit uncertainty—not merely the ability to generate an answer.

No invented opportunities

We won't fabricate colleges, fees, vacancies, deadlines or admission requirements.

Sources matter

Factual information should be traceable to the appropriate source.

Uncertainty stays visible

Unknown, outdated and unverified information must not be presented as confirmed fact.

Eligibility is not a guess

Student recommendations must account for actual requirements and clearly explain missing evidence.

Building in the right order

From reliable data to meaningful guidance.

Each stage depends on the previous one. We will not advertise a capability as available until it has actually been built and tested.

01

Establish the data layer

Identify legitimate sources, obtain real datasets, validate records and preserve provenance.

02

Build student profiles

Implement authentication, secure profile storage and relevant student preferences.

03

Implement matching

Evaluate qualifications, requirements, constraints and verified eligibility information.

04

Add AI guidance

Use source-backed information to explain choices, answer questions and help students plan next steps.
